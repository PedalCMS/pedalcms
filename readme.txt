Market your academic programs. Add program listings, a course catalog and a faculty & staff directory to your WordPress site.

== Description ==

Pedal CMS turns WordPress into a program marketing site for colleges, universities, schools and training providers. It gives prospective students one place to find everything about a program: what it covers, what it costs, who teaches it and how to apply.

Everything is managed from the familiar WordPress dashboard. You don't need any other plugins, page builders, or custom code.

### Program Listings

* A searchable, filterable program finder. Visitors can filter by keyword, program type, instruction mode (online, hybrid, in-person), college and department.
* Detailed program pages with an overview, prerequisites and clear "Request Info", "Apply Now" and "Contact Us" buttons.
* Application deadlines (Fall, Spring, etc.) that can be set site-wide or for each program.
* Related contacts so visitors know who to reach out to.

### Program Subpages

Each program gets its own set of tabbed subpages. Turn them on site-wide and hide any of them on individual programs:

* **Curriculum**: organize courses into sections, with optional credit counts.
* **Careers**: highlight where the program can lead.
* **Faculty & Staff**: show the people behind the program, optionally grouped by category.
* **Cost**: share estimated costs and tuition details.
* **How to Apply**: walk applicants through the process.
* **FAQs**: answer common questions, grouped by category if you like.
* **News**: keep program news in one place.

You can rename every subpage's title and tab label to match your institution's language.

### Course Catalog

* A searchable course catalog with its own filters.
* Course pages with a short description, course code, credits, instructors and a "Register" button that links to your registration system.
* Organize courses by subject and session.
* Rename labels such as "Credits" and "Instructors" to suit your terminology.

### Faculty & Staff Directory

* A browsable, filterable directory of the people at your institution.
* Profile pages with photo, job title, phone, email and office location.
* Group people by category, college and department.
* Connect people to the courses they teach. The link shows up on both the profile and the course page.

### Organize Your Way

Programs, courses and people can be organized by **College**, **Department**, **Program Type**, **Instruction Mode**, **Subject**, **Session** and **Personnel Category**. You can switch each grouping on or off and rename it to match your institution (for example "School" instead of "College"), and give each one its own archive page.

These taxonomies can be used to filter your content on the front end.

### Looks Good With Your Theme

Pick how much styling Pedal CMS adds:

* **Tuxedo**: a polished, ready-to-use look. Recommended for most sites.
* **Stealth**: just enough styling for the layout, so it blends in with your theme.
* **Ghost**: no styling at all, for full control.

Set your brand's accent color, choose the header image size, and turn breadcrumbs on or off.

**Note:** to make sure breadcrumbs are consistent across your entire site, Pedal CMS does not include breadcrumb functionality. Instead, it supports the following third party plugins:

* Breadcrumb NavXT
* Yoast SEO
* All in One SEO
* RankMath

== Installation ==

### From your WordPress dashboard

1. Go to Plugins > Add New
2. Search for 'Pedal CMS'
3. Click Install Now and then Activate
4. Follow the setup wizard to configure courses, payments, and pages

### Using FTP

1. Download `pedalcms.zip` and extract it on your computer.
2. Upload the `pedalcms` folder to your site's `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
3. Activate **Pedal CMS** from the **Plugins** screen.

### Getting started

1. After activation, a new **Pedal** menu appears in your dashboard.
2. Open **Pedal > Settings** to choose a Presentation Mode, set your brand colors, and configure the features you need: course catalog, directory and program subpages.
3. Start adding programs, courses, and personnel.
4. View them on the front end at `/programs/`, `/courses/`, and `/directory/`, respectively.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

### Who is Pedal CMS for?

Colleges, universities, community colleges, schools, bootcamps and any organization that offers programs or courses and wants to promote them online.

### Do I need any other plugins?

If you'd like breadcrumbs on your site, Pedal CMS supports several third party options: Breadcrumb NavXT, Yoast SEO, All in One SEO, and RankMath.

### Will it work with my theme?

Pedal CMS is designed to work with any WordPress theme. If its styling clashes with your theme, switch the Presentation Mode to **Stealth** or **Ghost** under **Programs > Settings**.

### Do I have to use the course catalog and directory?

No. Both are optional. They are enabled by default but you can turn them off under ** Pedal > Settings ** on their respective tabs.

### Can I change the wording, like "College" or "Credits"?

Yes. Many labels, button texts, subpage titles and tab names can be changed from the settings page,  ** Pedal > Settings **. Every label can be overridden using hooks.

### Can I hide a subpage on just one program?

Yes. Subpages are switched on site-wide in the settings, and you can disable any of them individual programs in the program edit screen.

### Is Pedal CMS a Learning Management System (LMS)?

No. It is strictly for marketing your course catalog, not facilitating coursework.

### Where can I get help?

Visit [pedalcms.com](https://pedalcms.com) or post in the [support forum](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/pedalcms/).
